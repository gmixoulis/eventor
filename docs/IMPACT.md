# Impact

## Why an event app matters

At most events the program lives in a PDF, the map is a printout and the people worth meeting are hidden in a crowd. Attendees spend effort on logistics (what is on now, where is it, how far) instead of conversations. An event app puts the schedule, map and attendee list in one place that updates live, so that effort drops.

## How it makes networking easier

Networking fails mostly for practical reasons: you do not know who is there, you do not know who is relevant, and arranging a meeting costs more effort than it is worth. Eventor targets each of those.

- **Matching by interests.** Profiles are compared with on-device embeddings, so the app can suggest people who share your interests and topics. It replaces scanning a long list by hand. Matching runs on the phone, so profile text is not sent to a server for this.
- **Directory.** A searchable list of attendees means you can look up a speaker or a person you were told to meet, instead of hunting for a badge.
- **1:1 meetings.** You can propose a time, and the other person accepts or declines. Pending and accepted states make it clear what is confirmed. This turns "we should talk" into a slot on both agendas.
- **In-app chat.** A message channel lets people coordinate and follow up without swapping personal contact details first.
- **QR-driven serendipity.** Scanning checkpoint and check-in QR codes brings attendees to different places, and the passport and leaderboard give a light reason to visit sessions and booths they would otherwise skip. More shared places means more chance encounters.
- **Assistant for "where and when".** Much of the friction at an event is simple questions: when does this start, which room, how do I get there. An assistant that answers from the event's facts keeps people from missing sessions or arriving late to a meeting. It is grounded only on the event data, so it should not invent details.

## What it does not do

It does not guarantee good conversations. Matching quality depends on how much attendees put into their profiles, and meetings only happen if people use the feature. The assistant is only as accurate as the event data you give it.

## Who benefits

- **Attendees** spend less time on logistics and find relevant people faster.
- **Organizers** get a reusable base for their own event instead of building from zero, and can edit the data in one place.
- **Speakers and exhibitors** are easier to find and to book time with.
